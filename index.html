Privacy Policy

SaveNext is designed to help you save WhatsApp notification messages, viewed WhatsApp statuses, and social media downloads. Your privacy is important to us, and we aim to keep your recovered content under your control.

Privacy First

SaveNext helps you save WhatsApp notification messages, viewed WhatsApp statuses, and social media downloads. Recovered WhatsApp messages are stored on your device.

Notification Access

When you allow notification access, SaveNext reads future WhatsApp notifications so it can keep a local copy if a message is deleted later. SaveNext cannot recover messages from before notification access was enabled.

Status Saver

SaveNext can show WhatsApp statuses that WhatsApp has already downloaded locally on your phone. It cannot access unseen statuses that are stored only on WhatsApp's servers.

Social Downloads

Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook links are used only when you paste a link and start a download. SaveNext uses the provided link to fetch the media you requested.

Files and Storage

Downloaded media is saved to your device's downloads or media folders. Folder access that you choose is used only to find local WhatsApp status files and save selected media.

Your Control

You can remove notification access from Android settings at any time. You can also delete saved media and recovered message records from your device whenever you no longer need them.

Data Storage

Recovered WhatsApp messages and downloaded media are stored locally on your device as described above. SaveNext is designed around keeping this content under your control on your device.

Third-Party Services

SaveNext may interact with third-party platforms such as WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook when you use features related to their notifications, statuses, or shared media links. SaveNext does not claim ownership of content belonging to these platforms or their users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be made available through the app or this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how SaveNext handles your data, please contact the app developer through the contact details provided with the application.
